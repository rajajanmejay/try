About the Club

WHERE SCIENCE
MEETS SPORT

Founded alongside one of India's most prestigious research institutions, the IISc Badminton Club brings together students, faculty, and staff in a shared passion for one of the world's fastest racket sports.

🏟️

World-Class Facilities

6 indoor courts with professional flooring, lighting, and nets — maintained to international standards.

🏆

Competitive Tournaments

Singles, doubles, and mixed events hosted year-round with open divisions for all skill levels.

🎓

Expert Coaching

Regular coaching sessions led by certified coaches and state-level players within IISc.
